A letter for Deepu

Dear Deepu,

Happy Birthday! ❤️

I wish I could be there in Kakinada today, see your birthday smile and celebrate this day with you. Since I can't be there in person, I wanted to make something that carries a little bit of my affection all the way from Hyderabad.

One thing I want you to remember: I admire you for being you. Your independence, your maturity, your emotions, your thoughts and the way you see the world — all of that deserves respect. I don't want you to ever feel that you need to make yourself smaller or change who you are to fit into anyone's expectations.

I love the big moments, but I also adore the small ones: the random talks, the silly reactions, the smiles, the laughter and the memories that we probably didn't realise would mean so much later.

Manam always happy ga undali ani kaadu — real ga, honest ga, understanding tho undali. We should be able to laugh together, talk openly, respect each other's space and support each other's dreams. That's what I value.

For this new year of your life, I wish you confidence in every decision, peace in your heart, exciting opportunities, good health, and countless reasons to feel proud of yourself. May you keep growing into everything you want to become.

And today, please take a moment to celebrate yourself. You deserve that — completely.

Happy Birthday, Deepu. With lots of love. ♥

Made with care, from Hyderabad to Kakinada. ♥
